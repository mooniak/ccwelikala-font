CCWelikala is a round, monoline Sinhala typeface by Damith Welikala (Ceylon Calligraphy), made in 2019. Its letters are drawn with one even line and round, open shapes, for headings, posters, packaging and short texts at larger sizes.

The font was first made in the FM Abhaya ASCII layout, the encoding most Sinhala fonts used before Unicode: Sinhala letters were drawn on Latin keys, so text only looked like Sinhala in that one font. In 2026 Mooniak ported it to Unicode. Every glyph now sits on its Sinhala code point or is reached through OpenType rules, the vowel signs that were typed as separate keys are composed, and the signs are positioned exactly where the original drew them.

The font covers the Sinhala letters in common use with their vowel signs, the repaya, rakaransaya and yansaya, ක්‍ෂ, ක්‍ව, ත්‍ව, න්‍ධ, ද්‍ර and the designer's own ර්‍ය, a set of consonant + vowel-sign ligatures, plus digits and basic punctuation, in a single Regular weight.

To contribute, see github.com/mooniak/ccwelikala-font.
